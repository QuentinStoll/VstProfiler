# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

The VSTProfiler community aims to be welcoming and respectful to contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Participation in issues, pull requests, reviews, and project discussions is subject to this code.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and open to different perspectives.
- Give constructive feedback about the work and accept corrections respectfully.
- Respect people's identities, boundaries, privacy, and time.
- Help keep discussions relevant to the project and accessible to newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption or retaliation against someone who raises a concern.

## Reporting

The proposed private contact for conduct concerns is Quentin at **`quentin.stoll@epitech.eu` (PLACEHOLDER - unconfirmed reporting address)**. Confirm this address and its use for conduct reports before publication or sending sensitive information.

Until the address is confirmed, use GitHub's abuse-reporting tools for behavior on GitHub, where applicable, or request a private maintainer contact without disclosing personal details or allegations publicly. If Quentin is involved in the complaint, request another maintainer or use GitHub's reporting tools where applicable.

Maintainers should verify the reporting address before inviting wider community participation. Anyone handling a report should limit access to those needed to assess and address it; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, request a change in behavior, or restrict participation according to the circumstances. Enforcement should be proportionate and explained to the affected participant where appropriate. A maintainer involved in a complaint should not decide its outcome alone.
